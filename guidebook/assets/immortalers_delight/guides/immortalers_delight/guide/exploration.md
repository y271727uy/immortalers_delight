# Exploration and Archaeology

Sniffers can dig up ancient plant material in different biomes. Bring them through several environments to discover the seeds described on the [Crops](crops.md) page.

## Archaeological Sites

The mod overview describes two new structures:

- **Abandoned Plains Residence** — a small plains structure containing suspicious gravel. It can provide Ancient Wood and has a chance to contain a Sniffer Egg.
- **Everlasting Cherry Tree** — a medium-sized structure found in biomes such as jungles and taigas. Archaeology there can reliably provide Sniffer Eggs.

Use a brush on suspicious blocks to uncover their contents. The mod also adds suspicious ash piles and other brushable blocks. Loot depends on the block and structure.

## Sniffer Brush

Immortalers Delight adds the Sniffer Fur Brush. Check its tooltip and JEI entry for the exact recipe and behavior in your installed version.
