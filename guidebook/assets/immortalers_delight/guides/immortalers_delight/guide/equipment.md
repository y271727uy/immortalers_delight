# Equipment

The mod adds antique blades, knives, a repeating crossbow, and utility items. Their recipes and combat values can be checked in JEI and the item tooltips.

## Sniffer Equipment

The Sniffer Saddle lets you equip a Sniffer. A saddle upgrade can provide the upgraded appearance and function. The Grain Sachet attracts Sniffers, while the Blazing Essential Oil resets their digging cooldown.

The Ancient Boat can transport a Sniffer. The Ancient Canopy Boat provides 54 inventory slots, as described by the mod overview.

## Other Items

The Ancient Alfalfa Loaf can block incoming damage. Other notable additions include the Sniffer Fur Brush and climbing rope.
