# Effects and Supernatural Mode

Immortalers Delight adds beneficial and harmful effects, along with several special states. Foods, drinks, and other items may grant different effects. Read each item's tooltip for details and use JEI to inspect its recipe.

## Supernatural Mode

Supernatural Mode substantially strengthens buff effects. The mod overview describes it as an option for modpacks with additional progression systems such as accessories or skill trees. The mode is controlled by the mod's configuration. Check the installed version's configuration and in-game descriptions for exact behavior.

Some effects have additional behavior at higher levels or in Supernatural Mode. Their details are best checked in the effect tooltip and the relevant item entry.
