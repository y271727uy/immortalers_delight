# Cooking and Food

Immortalers Delight adds a large selection of dishes and drinks made from its crops and other ingredients. Farmer's Delight cooking methods remain useful for many recipes. Look up individual ingredients in JEI to see the recipe and supported cooking method.

## Enchanted Cooler

The Enchanted Cooler prepares food and can also apply potions to the Pillager Knife. Its recipe is shapeless. Put Lapis Lazuli in the energy slot to charge it. A full charge consumes three Lapis Lazuli, and processing recipes use energy. Put ingredients in from the top, containers or fuel items in through the designated side slots, and collect products below. Recipes that use containers return the empty container through the top.

Use JEI to check the Enchanted Cooler recipes. Automation faces are split between top input, side container and energy slots, and bottom output.

## Ancient Stove

The Ancient Stove cooks up to six items like a Campfire when its top is clear. It also supplies heat to the Cooking Pot and Skillet. Water and rain do not extinguish it. Use a water bucket to put it out, then Flint and Steel to relight it.

The stove oxidizes over time. It works normally until fully oxidized, when its flame goes out and it no longer supplies heat. Scrape it with an Axe, as you would a Copper Block, to remove oxidation.

## Grudge Spring and Onsen Eggs

Plant Wraith Cinnamon on Soul Sand and place water around the crop. When the crop naturally matures, nearby water can turn into Grudge Spring fluid. The exact fluid recipes, including Onsen Eggs, are shown in the Grudge Spring category in JEI.

## Drinks

The mod adds teas, drinks, and alcoholic beverages. Some drinks can be placed in the world by right-clicking. Check the item's tooltip and JEI entry for serving and recipe details.

## Serving Food

Some dishes are placed as food blocks and can be shared by interacting with them. The behavior varies by dish. Use JEI and the item's tooltip to check portions and ingredients.
