# Ancient Crops

Many seeds are found when Sniffers dig in particular biomes. The biome list below follows the mod overview. Planting requirements differ between species, so check the item description and JEI for the current version.

| Crop | Where its seed or fruit is found | Growing notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evolutcorn | Plains | Plant on farmland. |
| Pearlip | Jungle | A three-block fruit tree grown on dirt-type blocks. |
| Himekaido | Forest | Plant on farmland. The shrub can be encouraged to grow into a tree. |
| Leisamboo | Rivers | An aquatic plant grown on dirt-type blocks beneath water. |
| Kwat Wheat | Crimson Forest | Plant on farmland, soul sand, or soul soil. Mature plants can release dangerous gas. |
| Ancient Alfalfa | Various terrain | Plant on farmland. It spreads like a wild grass. |
| Wraith Cinnamon | Warped Forest | When grown on soul sand or soul soil, a mature plant can turn nearby water into a hot spring. |
| Travastruggler | Mountain biomes | Plant on dirt-type blocks. |
| A Bush | Nether Wastes | Plant on netherrack. Mature plants can consume nearby lava and become A Bush wood. |
| Gelpitaya | Ice Spikes | Plant the seed on top of a cactus. A mature plant can release a small snowstorm. |
| Oxygen Grapes | Ocean Monument | An aquatic plant that grows underwater. |
| Sextlotus | Forest | Plant on farmland. It consumes nutrients as it grows and can degrade the soil. |
| Obsidian Walnut | Basalt Deltas | Plant on netherrack, soul sand, or soul soil. It grows slowly. |

The mod overview also lists a plains residence and a cherry grove tree ruin as archaeological locations. See [Exploration](exploration.md) for the structures and Sniffer digging.
