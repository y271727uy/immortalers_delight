# Immortalers Delight

Welcome to the Immortalers Delight guidebook. This mod expands Farmer's Delight with ancient crops, unusual ingredients, archaeological sites, equipment, and new ways to prepare food.

## Getting Started

Sniffers are the key to finding many of the mod's ancient seeds. Take them to different biomes and let them dig. The [Crops](crops.md) page summarizes the plants and their growing conditions.

Explore the [archaeological sites](exploration.md) for suspicious blocks and other discoveries. Once you have ingredients, prepare dishes with Farmer's Delight cooking blocks or the [Enchanted Cooler](cooking.md).

The mod also adds [equipment and Sniffer tools](equipment.md), along with food effects and the optional [Supernatural Mode](effects.md).
